COVID-19 and pregnancy

🏠 Home / Infections and vaccines / Travel precautions

💡 What You Need to Know

  • Increased Risks: Pregnant individuals are at higher risk for severe COVID-19 illness, including hospitalization, ICU admission, mechanical ventilation, and death, compared to non-pregnant individuals.
  • Pregnancy Complications: COVID-19 during pregnancy is associated with an increased risk of adverse outcomes such as preterm birth, preeclampsia, stillbirth, and admission to neonatal intensive care for the newborn.
  • Vaccination is Key: COVID-19 vaccination is strongly recommended for pregnant individuals to protect both the mother and the baby, offering significant protection against severe disease.
  • Travel Considerations: International and domestic travel during pregnancy, especially during periods of high community transmission, increases exposure risk to SARS-CoV-2.

🤒 Associated Symptoms

  • Common Symptoms: Fever or chills, cough, shortness of breath, fatigue, muscle or body aches, headache, sore throat, congestion or runny nose, nausea or vomiting, and diarrhea.
  • Severe Symptoms: Difficulty breathing, persistent pain or pressure in the chest, new confusion, inability to wake or stay awake, pale, gray, or blue-colored skin, lips, or nail beds, depending on skin tone.
  • Pregnancy-Specific Concerns: Any new onset of severe headache, vision changes, abdominal pain, or swelling in the hands/face should be evaluated promptly, as these can overlap with preeclampsia symptoms.
  • Fetal Movement Changes: A significant decrease or absence of fetal movement warrants immediate medical evaluation, especially if experiencing other COVID-19 symptoms.

🛡 Crucial Precautions

  • COVID-19 Vaccination: Ensure up-to-date vaccination, including booster doses, as recommended by health authorities, ideally before or early in pregnancy.
  • Masking and Distancing: Consistently wear a well-fitting mask in indoor public settings and maintain physical distance from others, especially when community transmission is high or when traveling.
  • Hand Hygiene: Practice frequent handwashing with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, or use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol if soap and water are unavailable.
  • Avoid Crowds: Minimize exposure to large gatherings and crowded indoor spaces, particularly during travel or in areas with high viral activity.
  • Pre-Travel Testing: Consider SARS-CoV-2 testing before and after travel, especially if visiting areas with higher transmission rates or if experiencing any symptoms.
  • Isolation Protocols: Understand and adhere to isolation and quarantine guidelines if exposed to or infected with COVID-19, even while traveling.

🍽 Dietary Directions & Restrictions

  • Maintain Hydration: Ensure adequate fluid intake, especially when traveling or if experiencing fever, to prevent dehydration which can be more critical during pregnancy.
  • Nutrient-Rich Diet: Focus on a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains to support immune function and overall health during pregnancy.
  • Food Safety During Travel: Adhere to strict food safety practices, consuming only cooked foods, peeled fruits, and bottled water to avoid foodborne illnesses that could complicate a COVID-19 infection.
  • Avoid Sugary Drinks: Limit intake of sugary beverages and highly processed foods, which can contribute to inflammation and may not support optimal immune response.

⚠️ Attendant Guidelines

  • Monitor for Symptoms: Attendants and family members should vigilantly monitor the pregnant individual for any signs or symptoms of COVID-19 or pregnancy complications.
  • Emergency Preparedness: Know the location of the nearest medical facility and have a plan for emergency transport, especially when traveling.
  • Support Isolation: If the pregnant individual tests positive, support strict isolation measures to prevent further spread within the household or travel group.
  • Travel Insurance: Ensure comprehensive travel insurance that covers medical emergencies, including COVID-19 related care and potential early return, is in place before any travel.
  • Local Health Guidance: Be aware of and comply with local health guidelines and restrictions at the travel destination, including testing and quarantine requirements.

🩺 Physician's Perspective

  • Personalized Risk Assessment: Discuss travel plans with an obstetrician to assess individual risks based on gestational age, underlying health conditions, and destination-specific COVID-19 prevalence.
  • Vaccination Status Review: Confirm the pregnant patient's COVID-19 vaccination status and recommend appropriate doses or boosters prior to travel.
  • Symptom Management Plan: Develop a clear plan for symptom monitoring and immediate medical contact if COVID-19 symptoms or pregnancy complications arise during or after travel.
  • Post-Travel Monitoring: Advise on continued self-monitoring for symptoms for at least 10-14 days post-travel and consider testing, even if asymptomatic.
  • Mental Health Support: Acknowledge and address any anxiety or stress related to COVID-19 and travel during pregnancy, offering appropriate support or referrals.

🎓 Academic & Nursing Corner

  • Patient Education: Educate pregnant patients on the heightened risks of COVID-19 during pregnancy and the importance of vaccination and preventative measures, especially concerning travel.
  • Travel Counseling: Provide comprehensive counseling on safe travel practices, including mask use, hand hygiene, avoiding crowded spaces, and understanding destination-specific health advisories.
  • Symptom Recognition: Train on recognizing both common COVID-19 symptoms and potential pregnancy-specific complications that may mimic or be exacerbated by the virus.
  • Resource Provision: Equip patients with reliable resources for up-to-date information on COVID-19 and pregnancy from reputable health organizations.
  • Post-Travel Follow-up: Schedule or advise on post-travel follow-up appointments to monitor maternal and fetal well-being.

🔬 Clinical Reference Index

  • CDC Guidelines: Refer to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) recommendations for COVID-19 and pregnancy, including travel advisories.
  • WHO Recommendations: Consult World Health Organization (WHO) guidance on COVID-19 in pregnancy and international travel health.
  • ACOG Statements: Review American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) practice advisories and statements on COVID-19 vaccination and management in pregnant individuals.
  • SARS-CoV-2 Variants: Stay informed on emerging SARS-CoV-2 variants and their potential impact on pregnant populations and vaccine efficacy.
  • Maternal-Fetal Transmission: Understand current research on the risk and implications of vertical (maternal-fetal) transmission of SARS-CoV-2.
