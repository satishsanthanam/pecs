# 🩺 Medical Content

## 💡 What You Need to Know
* **Immediate Safety & Support:** Prioritize your safety, seek a secure environment, and reach out to trusted individuals or crisis hotlines for immediate support.
* **Medical Evaluation:** Crucial for addressing physical injuries, preventing sexually transmitted infections (STIs), and accessing emergency contraception.
* **Forensic Examination (SANE/SAFE):** An optional but important step to collect evidence, ideally within 72-120 hours. Avoid showering, changing clothes, or cleaning the assault site if evidence collection is desired.
* **Emotional & Psychological Impact:** Acknowledge the profound trauma; seeking counseling and mental health support is a vital part of recovery.


## 🤒 Associated Symptoms
* **Physical Injuries:** Presence of bruises, lacerations, fractures, internal trauma, or genital injuries requiring medical attention.
* **Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs):** New onset of genital discharge, pain, itching, sores, or asymptomatic exposure necessitating screening and prophylactic treatment.
* **Unwanted Pregnancy:** Concerns regarding potential conception, missed menstrual periods, nausea, or breast tenderness.
* **Psychological Trauma:** Manifestations such as acute stress reaction, anxiety, depression, flashbacks, sleep disturbances, fear, shame, or guilt.
* **Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs):** Symptoms including dysuria (painful urination), increased frequency, urgency, or hematuria (blood in urine).


## 🛡️ Crucial Precautions
* **Evidence Preservation:** If considering forensic evidence collection, avoid showering, bathing, douching, changing clothes, or cleaning the assault site until after the examination.
* **STI Prophylaxis Adherence:** Strictly follow the prescribed regimen for post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) for HIV and other STIs to minimize infection risk.
* **Emergency Contraception Timeliness:** Administer emergency contraception within the recommended timeframe (typically 72-120 hours) to prevent unwanted pregnancy.
* **Follow-up Care Compliance:** Attend all scheduled appointments for repeat STI testing, pregnancy testing, and ongoing mental health support.
* **Safety Planning:** Develop a comprehensive safety plan, especially if the assailant is known or there is a perceived ongoing threat.


## 🍽️ Dietary Directions & Restrictions
* **Hydration:** Maintain adequate fluid intake to support overall physical well-being, especially during periods of stress or if taking medications that may cause dehydration.
* **Nutritional Support:** Consume easily digestible, nutrient-dense foods to aid physical recovery and sustain energy levels, particularly if appetite is suppressed due to trauma.
* **Medication Adherence with Food:** Take any prescribed medications (e.g., antibiotics, anti-nausea) with food as directed by a healthcare professional to minimize gastrointestinal upset.
* **Avoidance of Substances:** Limit or avoid alcohol and illicit drugs, as they can interfere with medication efficacy, impair judgment, and exacerbate psychological distress.


## ⚠️ Attendant Guidelines
* **Believe and Validate:** Listen without judgment, validate the survivor's experience, and unequivocally assure them that the assault was not their fault.
* **Offer Practical Support:** Assist with practical needs such as transportation to medical facilities, contacting support services, or ensuring a safe and secure environment.
* **Respect Autonomy:** Empower the survivor by allowing them to make their own decisions regarding reporting, medical care, and subsequent steps at their own pace.
* **Maintain Confidentiality:** Uphold strict confidentiality regarding their experience and do not share any information without their explicit consent.
* **Facilitate Professional Guidance:** Encourage and actively facilitate access to medical professionals, counselors, and legal advocates specializing in sexual assault support.


## 🩺 Physician's Perspective
* **Comprehensive Medical Evaluation:** Conduct a thorough physical examination, including assessment for injuries, comprehensive STI screening, and evaluation of pregnancy risk.
* **Prophylactic Treatment:** Offer immediate post-exposure prophylaxis for STIs (e.g., HIV, chlamydia, gonorrhea) and provide emergency contraception as indicated.
* **Trauma-Informed Care:** Approach the patient with profound empathy, respect, and sensitivity, ensuring a safe, non-judgmental, and empowering clinical environment.
* **Meticulous Documentation:** Accurately and objectively document all findings, interventions, patient statements, and referrals in the medical record.
* **Referral Services:** Provide clear and actionable referrals to sexual assault nurse examiners (SANE/SAFE), mental health professionals, and victim advocacy services.


## 🎓 Academic & Nursing Corner
* **Trauma-Informed Approach:** Prioritize patient comfort, privacy, and a sense of control throughout the examination and interview process.
* **Evidence Collection Protocol:** Understand and meticulously follow SANE/SAFE protocols for forensic evidence collection, ensuring proper chain of custody.
* **STI & Pregnancy Screening Education:** Educate patients comprehensively on the importance of STI testing, treatment options, and emergency contraception.
* **Psychosocial Support & Assessment:** Assess for signs of acute stress, anxiety, and depression, providing immediate emotional support and appropriate referrals to mental health services.
* **Resource Navigation:** Be knowledgeable about local, national, and online resources available for sexual assault survivors, including crisis hotlines, counseling, and legal aid.


## 🔬 Clinical Reference Index
* **SANE/SAFE Nurse:** Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner / Sexual Assault Forensic Examiner, specialized in forensic evidence collection and trauma-informed care.
* **PEP:** Post-Exposure Prophylaxis, a short-term antiretroviral treatment to reduce the likelihood of HIV infection after potential exposure.
* **EC:** Emergency Contraception, medication used to prevent pregnancy after unprotected sexual intercourse or contraceptive failure.
* **CDC Guidelines:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention guidelines for the medical management of sexual assault, including STI and HIV prophylaxis.
* **RAINN:** Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network, the largest anti-sexual violence organization in the U.S., operating a national hotline.
* **Forensic Evidence Kit:** A standardized collection kit used by trained professionals to gather physical evidence following a sexual assault.
