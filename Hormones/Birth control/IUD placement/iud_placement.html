IUD placement

🏠 Home / Hormones / Birth control

💡 What You Need to Know

  • Intrauterine Device (IUD): A highly effective, long-acting reversible contraceptive (LARC) method.
  • Types Available: Includes hormonal IUDs (releasing progestin) and non-hormonal copper IUDs.
  • Mechanism of Action: Hormonal IUDs primarily thicken cervical mucus and thin the uterine lining; copper IUDs create an inflammatory reaction toxic to sperm and eggs.
  • Duration of Efficacy: Provides contraception for 3 to 10 years, depending on the specific type of IUD.

🤒 Associated Symptoms

  • Cramping and Discomfort: Common during and immediately after the insertion procedure.
  • Spotting or Irregular Bleeding: May occur in the initial months post-insertion, particularly with hormonal IUDs.
  • Vasovagal Response: Some individuals may experience dizziness, lightheadedness, or nausea during insertion.
  • Increased Menstrual Bleeding/Cramps: Copper IUDs can sometimes lead to heavier periods and more cramping, especially in the first few cycles.

🛡 Crucial Precautions

  • Pregnancy Exclusion: IUD placement is contraindicated if pregnancy is suspected or confirmed.
  • Active Pelvic Infection: Presence of an active sexually transmitted infection (STI) or pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) is a contraindication.
  • Uterine Anomalies: Significant uterine fibroids or structural abnormalities may complicate insertion or efficacy.
  • Allergy to Components: A known allergy to copper (for copper IUDs) or other device materials is a contraindication.
  • Post-Insertion Monitoring: Watch for signs of infection (fever, severe pain), expulsion, or uterine perforation.

🍽 Dietary Directions & Restrictions

  • No Specific Fasting Required: Generally, no dietary restrictions are necessary before IUD placement.
  • Light Meal Recommended: Eating a light meal prior to the appointment can help prevent lightheadedness or nausea.
  • Hydration: Ensure adequate fluid intake before and after the procedure.
  • Avoid Alcohol/Caffeine (Optional): Some providers may suggest avoiding these immediately before if the patient is prone to anxiety or vasovagal responses.

⚠️ Attendant Guidelines

  • Pain Management Discussion: Discuss pain relief options with your healthcare provider before the procedure.
  • Post-Procedure Rest: Plan for a period of rest immediately after insertion, as cramping is common.
  • String Check Education: Learn how to regularly check for IUD strings to ensure proper placement.
  • Report Complications Promptly: Immediately report severe pain, heavy bleeding, fever, foul-smelling discharge, or inability to feel the IUD strings.

🩺 Physician's Perspective

  • Thorough Pre-Screening: Essential to rule out contraindications such as pregnancy, active STIs, or uterine abnormalities.
  • Comprehensive Counseling: Discuss IUD types, benefits, risks, potential side effects, and expected post-insertion experience with the patient.
  • Pain Management Strategy: Consider prophylactic NSAIDs or local anesthetic to minimize discomfort during insertion.
  • Follow-up Care: Schedule a follow-up visit, typically 4-6 weeks post-insertion, to confirm placement and address any patient concerns.

🎓 Academic & Nursing Corner

  • Patient Education: Provide clear instructions on pre-procedure preparation, what to expect during insertion, and post-procedure care.
  • Pain Assessment & Management: Assess patient's pain levels before, during, and after insertion; administer prescribed analgesics as needed.
  • Vitals Monitoring: Monitor vital signs, especially during and immediately after insertion, for signs of vasovagal response.
  • Post-Procedure Counseling: Educate on string checking, signs of complications, and when to seek immediate medical attention.

🔬 Clinical Reference Index

  • Types: Levonorgestrel-releasing intrauterine systems (LNG-IUS) and Copper Intrauterine Devices (Cu-IUD).
  • Mechanism (LNG-IUS): Localized progestin release causes endometrial thinning, increased cervical mucus viscosity, and impaired sperm motility/viability.
  • Mechanism (Cu-IUD): Induces a sterile inflammatory reaction in the endometrium, creating a spermicidal environment and preventing fertilization/implantation.
  • Efficacy: Pearl Index typically <1 for both types, indicating high effectiveness in preventing pregnancy.
